2.1 Access Customer will provide safe access to the work area during agreed working hours[cite: 16]. An adult age 18 or older who can make service decisions must be reachable by phone while FloorEver is on site[cite: 16].

2.2 Site Readiness Before the scheduled start, Customer will remove vehicles, stored items, debris, and other obstructions unless the Estimate states that FloorEver will move specified items for an additional charge[cite: 16]. 3 FloorEver Responsibilities

3.1 Workmanship FloorEver will perform the work in a professional manner using the system identified in the signed Estimate[cite: 17].

3.2 Surface Preparation Unless the signed Estimate states otherwise, FloorEver will mechanically prepare the concrete using diamond grinding to create a profile suitable for the coating application[cite: 17]. Acid etching is not the primary preparation method[cite: 17].

3.3 Dust Control FloorEver will use reasonable dust-control practices[cite: 17]. Mechanical preparation can still produce fine dust, including in adjacent spaces[cite: 17]. Customer must identify any area that requires special isolation before work begins so the parties can address it in the Estimate or a Service Change Authorization[cite: 17].

3.4 Communication FloorEver will notify Customer of hidden or unexpected conditions that may materially change the scope, price, appearance, or schedule before covering those conditions, when reasonably possible[cite: 17].

3.5 Cleanup At service completion, FloorEver will remove its equipment and service debris and leave the immediate work area reasonably clean[cite: 17]. Some residual fine dust from mechanical grinding may remain[cite: 17]. Disposal of Customer-owned items is not included unless the Estimate says otherwise[cite: 17].

3.6 Personnel FloorEver may use employees, coating crews, or other service providers under its supervision to perform the coating services[cite: 17].

4 Service Scope Concrete Conditions and Materials

4.1 Included Work The work is limited to the items written in the signed Estimate, including measured area, coating system, finish, surface preparation, repairs, vertical surfaces, and listed add-ons[cite: 17].

4.2 Excluded Work Unless listed in the signed Estimate, the price does not include moving contents, appliances, or vehicles; extensive spall or structural repair; moisture mitigation beyond a listed product; coves or vertical surfaces; work outside the measured area; specialized decorative work; hazardous-material testing or remediation; or night, weekend, or expedited work[cite: 17].

4.3 Hidden Conditions Concrete can conceal moisture, contamination, cracks, weak surface material, prior repair products, or a failing existing coating[cite: 17]. If FloorEver discovers a condition that requires services outside the signed Estimate, it may pause the affected area and propose a Service Change Authorization[cite: 17].

4.4 Moisture and Compatibility Moisture testing and mitigation are included only when stated in the signed Estimate[cite: 17]. No coating system eliminates all risk from future moisture, hydrostatic pressure, structural movement, or contamination beyond the product's design limits[cite: 17].

4.5 Materials FloorEver will use professional-grade products appropriate for the quoted system[cite: 17]. If supply conditions require a product substitution, FloorEver may use a product with reasonably comparable or better intended performance and warranty, provided the substitution does not materially change the sold system, appearance, or price without Customer's written approval[cite: 17].

4.6 Appearance Decorative coating is hand applied[cite: 17]. Samples, photographs, and small test areas show general color and texture but cannot guarantee identical flake distribution, pattern, gloss, or appearance under every lighting condition[cite: 17].

5 Insurance and Work Area Safety

5.1 Insurance FloorEver maintains commercial general liability insurance and will provide a certificate of insurance upon reasonable request[cite: 17].

5.2 Customer Property Customer remains responsible for the building, vehicles or belongings left in or near the work area, and security of the premises[cite: 17]. 6.3 Return to Service Service completion is not the same as full cure[cite: 18]. Customer will not allow foot traffic, vehicles, water, chemicals, or stored items on the floor until the applicable time stated in FloorEver's written care instructions[cite: 18].
